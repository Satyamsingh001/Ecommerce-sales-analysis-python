#  E-Commerce Sales Analysis Using Python

## Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales data using Python to uncover business insights related to revenue performance, customer purchasing behavior, payment preferences, geographic distribution, and growth trends.

The objective is to transform raw transactional data into meaningful insights that help businesses improve profitability, reduce risks, and make data-driven decisions.

---

##  Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Identify top-performing product categories.
- Evaluate regional revenue contribution.
- Study customer purchasing behavior.
- Analyze payment method preferences.
- Detect business risks and growth opportunities.

---

---

##  Key Business Insights

### Revenue Performance
- Electronics generated the highest revenue at **$15.6M**.
- Revenue showed consistent growth from **2020–2024**.

### Geographic Analysis
- United States contributed approximately **70% of total revenue**.
- Texas and California together generated nearly **43% of total sales**.

### Customer Behavior
- Average Order Value (AOV) reached **$918**, indicating a high-value customer base.

### Payment Analysis
- Credit Cards accounted for **35%** of transactions.
- UPI contributed **15%**.
- Cash on Delivery represented only **5%** of transactions.

---

##  Business Risks Identified

### Geographic Dependency
Heavy reliance on the US market increases exposure to regulatory, economic, and tariff-related risks.

### Order Processing Issues
Approximately 25% of orders remain cancelled or pending, resulting in lost revenue opportunities.

### Growth Optimization
Although revenue growth is stable, additional strategies are required to accelerate growth beyond current levels.

---

##  Recommendations

- Expand marketing efforts in Canada, India, and the UK.
- Improve fulfillment and processing speed for pending orders.
- Launch targeted campaigns during low-performing months.
- Increase promotional investment in Electronics products.
- Diversify revenue sources to reduce geographic concentration risk.
